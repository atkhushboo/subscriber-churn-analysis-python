# 📊 Fit.ly Customer Churn Analysis

A customer churn analysis project for **Fit.ly**, a subscription-based fitness platform.

Using Python, I explored customer account, support, and activity data to understand **where churn is happening and what might be driving it.**

---

## 🔍 Analysis

The analysis focuses on:

- Churn across different subscription plans
- Customer support topics and churn
- Support resolution time
- Customer activity and engagement
- Overall customer churn rate

---

## 📈 Key Findings

| Metric | Finding |
|---|---|
| Overall Churn Rate | **28.5%** |
| Highest Churn Plan | **Free (~41%)** |
| Highest Churn Support Topic | **Billing** |

The **Free plan** stands out with the highest churn rate, while **billing-related support issues** also show the highest churn among support topics.

These areas could be useful starting points for understanding and reducing customer churn.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, Fit.ly could focus on:

- Improving the experience of Free plan users
- Handling billing-related issues more effectively
- Reducing customer support resolution time
- Monitoring churn regularly to track whether retention improves
